About the project

Find jobs with real sponsorship evidence.

SponsorshipJobsUK collects current UK vacancies from public job sources, checks each employer against the official UK licensed sponsor register, and analyses the advert wording to surface genuine sponsorship opportunities — with direct application links.

What SponsorshipJobsUK does

SponsorshipJobsUK is a free, modern job search tool designed for people looking for UK roles that may offer visa sponsorship. Instead of browsing endless listings and guessing, you can search, filter and immediately see whether an employer appears on the official licensed sponsor register and whether the advert itself talks about sponsorship.

Every listing links back to the original publisher so you can apply directly. We never host applications ourselves and we never ask you to sign up.

✓Search current UK vacancies by job title, location and category
✓Filter to roles where the employer is a licensed sponsor and the advert confirms sponsorship
✓See the exact register-match status and sponsorship wording for every job
✓Apply directly through the original job advert
✓Results limited to the last 28 days by default, so listings stay fresh
✓Works on mobile, tablet and desktop with no app required

Where the jobs come from

We import vacancies from public job sources and combine them with the official UK Government sponsor register. Each source adds a different layer of evidence:

One of the largest public job boards in the UK. We use it to surface current vacancies across many industries and locations.

🏢

Glassdoor UK

Company listings and job adverts that often include extra context about the employer and the role.

📋

UK Government Sponsor Register

The official register of licensed sponsors published by the Home Office. We match every employer name against this list.

Additional public listings that help us cover more roles, sectors and regions across the UK.

How the sponsorship check works

Every job goes through a simple three-step check before it appears in your search results:

1

Employer name matched against the official register

We compare the employer name on the advert with the UK Government's register of licensed sponsors. Exact matches are flagged so you can see whether the company holds a sponsor licence.

2

Advert description analysed for sponsorship wording

We read the job description and highlight phrases such as "visa sponsorship available" or "we are a licensed sponsor". We also detect wording that says sponsorship is not offered, so those roles can be filtered out.

3

Results classified into clear statuses

Each role gets a status: register match + advert evidence (best), not stated or unconfirmed, or advert says unavailable. The default filter shows only the strongest matches first.

⚠️

A register match is not a guarantee. A company may hold a sponsor licence but still choose not to sponsor a particular vacancy. Always confirm sponsorship directly with the employer and check current Home Office rules before applying.

How search and filters work

When you use the search page, the site combines your filters with the imported job data and shows the most relevant results:

Search jobs queries the live job sources and pulls in current vacancies for your job title or location. Apply filters works on the already-imported listings, so it responds instantly and never leaves the page. Near me uses your device location to find roles within your chosen radius.

The default sponsorship filter shows only roles where the employer is a licensed sponsor and the advert explicitly mentions sponsorship. You can switch to "All jobs" or "No sponsorship offered" at any time to see a wider set of listings. Results are limited to the last 28 days by default, so you are not looking at stale adverts.

Privacy and responsibility

We do not require an account and we do not ask for your CV. Your searches stay in your browser, and applications are handled entirely by the original job publisher. SponsorshipJobsUK does not act as a recruiter and does not make immigration decisions.

Always verify sponsorship and eligibility against the official UK Government sponsor register and the latest Home Office guidance before making an application or accepting a role.
