Find UK Visa Sponsorship Jobs

SponsorshipJobsUK helps job seekers explore UK vacancies that may be relevant to people who need employer sponsorship. Search by job title, town or city, job category, posting date and other available filters. Listings link to their original sources so you can review the employer's requirements before applying.

Search jobs by role and sector

Use the job search to explore opportunities across sectors such as healthcare, care, information technology, engineering, education, hospitality and business services. Availability changes over time, and not every vacancy or employer offers visa sponsorship. Always read the original advert and confirm eligibility directly with the employer.

Check employer sponsorship evidence

Where available, this website compares employer information with the UK Register of Licensed Sponsors and reviews wording found in job adverts. A register match is not a guarantee that a specific vacancy offers sponsorship, that the employer will sponsor every applicant, or that an applicant qualifies for a visa. Check current official guidance and the employer's own recruitment information before making a decision.

How to search effectively

  • Enter a specific role, such as care assistant, nurse, software engineer or data analyst.
  • Add a UK town, city or postcode to narrow your search.
  • Use the posting-date and job-type filters to refine available results.
  • Open the original vacancy to confirm salary, duties, closing date and sponsorship conditions.
