Quick answers

Is SponsorshipJobsUK free?

Yes. Searching, filtering and checking the sponsor register are completely free and require no account.

Do you apply for jobs on my behalf?

No. Every listing links back to the original publisher, so you apply directly with the employer or job board.

Why do some results take longer to load?

The site is in active development. If a search feels slow or returns a gap, simply refresh the page — cached results restore instantly while the live feed updates.

How do I report a bug?

Use the form on this page and include the page name and what went wrong. Screenshots are always helpful.
